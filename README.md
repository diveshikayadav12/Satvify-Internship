# Satvify Internship Submission Pack

## Project

**Research & Analytics Internship -- Satvify**

**Prepared by:** Diveshika Yadav

## Included files

1.  **Satvify_Internship_Report.docx** -- final internship report with
    methodology, survey analysis, pie charts, findings, recommendations,
    conclusion and the 20 FAQ items supplied for the Satvify
    subscription plan.
2.  **Satvify_Internship_Report_Presentation.pptx** -- concise
    presentation version of the internship report for viva/presentation.
3.  **README.md** -- this file.

## Primary data

The report uses Google Forms survey results supplied for the internship.
The available survey sets are presented separately because their sample
sizes differ: - Customer Loyalty -- 22 responses - Customer Retention --
15 responses - Early-Stage Customer Acquisition -- 23 responses

The survey percentages are reproduced from the supplied survey analysis
and converted into pie charts.

## FAQ source

The FAQ section is based on the supplied Satvify subscription-plan FAQ
document. It covers Satvify's offering, ingredients/food positioning,
ordering, subscription plans, customization, best-selling items,
delivery, payments, contact, and other customer questions.

## Important note

The survey samples are small convenience samples. Findings are therefore
**directional internship-level insights**, not population estimates.
